# Surface: carte des cyclones actifs

Mode: operate. The visitor checks which tropical cyclones are active and where they are. The page keeps the map, the basin groups, the measurements already published, and the refresh. Code-led: no image generation in this harness.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This surface is a radiofacsimile just pulled from the machine and laid on the chart. It refuses the centered welcome, the logo, and the white card beside a boxed map.

OWN-WORLD: Cool thermal paper #e3e1da, ink #17191b, hairline #8d9088, and one pencil red #c5342a. Familjen Grotesk for names, Azeret Mono for wind, pressure, identifiers, and times. Square edges, 1px rules, the paper column casting an offset shadow onto the chart. Pencil red appears only on Actualiser and on the fix that is circled.

STORY: The visitor sees the oceans at once, reads the plotted fixes, and circles one storm to bring it into the open part of the chart.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A paper column, 26rem, holds the name Traqueur de Tempêtes, the Actualiser control at the top right of that column, the heading Tempêtes actives, and the fixes. Choosing a storm replaces that heading with the storm name at display scale, up to 4.25rem. The chart occupies the rest of the viewport and runs off the top, right, and bottom edges. Leaflet controls sit on the open chart as small paper stamps. Credits sit at the foot of the paper, not under the map.

FORM: Radiofacsimile weather chart, candidate 7 of the grounded list, seed 442c2739. Raises kept from declined challengers: specimen scale (the circled name is the display type); action-only accent (pencil red only where a press or a selection exists); shared baseline (every fix uses the same columns); bleed (the chart leaves the frame, the paper does not wrap it in a card).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
